# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Primary:** Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential collaborators evaluating Bunyasit as a builder and engineer
- **Secondary:** Fellow developers and friends exploring the site out of curiosity
- **Context:** Visitors arrive looking for evidence of technical skill, creativity, and personality — often in a single glance. The site must communicate competence and originality simultaneously.
- **Emotional goal:** Curiosity and delight. The visitor should feel like they've discovered something unexpected — a portfolio that doesn't behave like a portfolio.

## Product Purpose

bigf.me is a personal identity surface and portfolio for Bunyasit Fang. It exists to demonstrate technical craft (terminal interface, infinite canvas, custom cursor, CRT effects) while telling a personal story through life postcards and curated workspaces. Success means a visitor remembers the site after leaving it.

## Brand Personality

Intentional, curious, playful, creative, deep. A builder who cares about aesthetics as much as functionality. Not afraid to be technical and human at the same time.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with identical hero-metric layouts
- Corporate minimalist portfolios that strip all personality
- Template-driven sites (Webflow, Framer templates with no customization)
- Overly dense developer portfolios that look like documentation sites
- Glassmorphism-by-default aesthetics without purpose

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — The interface itself demonstrates technical capability; no need to list skills in a grid.
2. **Nostalgia with intent** — CRT effects, scanlines, and terminal chrome are deliberate atmosphere, not decoration.
3. **Playful depth** — Surface simplicity should invite exploration. Every interaction reveals more.
4. **Human first, technical second** — Life postcards and personal voice sit alongside engineering workspace.
5. **Own the aesthetic** — Vaporwave/synthwave is the lane. Commit fully rather than dabble.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — terminal effects and animations degrade gracefully
- Skip-intro functionality for the loading screen
- Terminal output remains keyboard-navigable
- Color contrast on terminal text meets WCAG 2.1 AA minimums
